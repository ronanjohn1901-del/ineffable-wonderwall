Dear Teacher,

I don't think "thank you" is always enough.

Thank you for the lessons, the explanations, the patience, the conversations, and all the moments that probably seemed ordinary at the time.

Some of those moments stayed with me longer than you probably realize.

So this isn't just a Teachers' Day message. It is simply me trying to put into words something that deserves to be said.

Thank you.

If I could thank you for one thing...

just one.

So,
thank you.

Thank you for every lesson, every conversation, every correction, every encouragement, and every moment you probably thought was ordinary.

I may not remember every chapter, every assignment, or every date. But I will remember what it felt like to have a teacher who cared.

Thank You.

For the lessons that ended with the bell — and the ones that didn't.
